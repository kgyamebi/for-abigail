A page for one person

Abigail Horlali Fiawoo

From Kofi Asante

Scroll

I made a quiet place
and put your name in it.

The night can keep the rest.
This part is yours.

If someone else is looking, let them.
They only get to visit.

1 October 2026

Abigail,

There are pages made for everyone. This one refused.

Your name is long enough to be read aloud. Abigail Horlali Fiawoo. I set it large because anything smaller would have been careless.

I do not have a speech hidden here. I have your face, a little time, and my name at the bottom so you know who stayed still long enough to make it.

Stay until the end. The last thing only appears if you hold on.

Kofi Asante
